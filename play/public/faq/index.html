Beauty of Chess

Analyze a position

Tap the knight on Home, then take a photo of a real board, choose a screenshot, or set up a board by hand. Stockfish 17 finds the best moves right on your phone.

Analyzing from a photo

  • Shoot the board from above, with all 64 squares in the picture and good light. Screenshots from other chess apps work too.
  • Reading takes up to a minute. Tap Stop to set the pieces up yourself instead.
  • The analysis opens by itself. Your photo sits at the top, so you can compare it with the board.
  • On the analysis, the camera and photo buttons at the lower right start a new board.

A piece is wrong or missing

Tap Edit pieces beside your photo. Pick a piece from the row under the board and tap a square to place it. The eraser takes a piece off. Tap Analyze when it looks right.

Rotate or Flip board?

  • Rotate (in Edit pieces) turns the position itself around. Use it when a photo was taken from the other side of the board and the pieces came out upside down.
  • Flip board (the top right of the analysis) only turns your view. The position stays the same, so you can look at it from Black's side.

It's the wrong side to move

Tap White to move (or Black) under the board to give the move to the other side. The analysis starts over. In Edit pieces you can also choose it with the White / Black switch.

Reading the lines

  • The bar and the number show who is better. Plus is good for White, minus is good for Black.
  • Tap a line to play its move on the board, or tap a piece to try your own move.
  • Play the line plays the best moves out one at a time. The arrows beside it step back and ahead.

Tilted or Flat

The switch under the board shows the carved 3D pieces tilted, or the board flat from above. Your choice is kept everywhere in the app.
