Privacy Policy

Beauty of Chess · Last updated 8 November 2026

The short version

Beauty of Chess has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your games, progress and settings stay on your iPhone. A few features need a server to work; what they send is used only for that feature and is not tied to who you are.

What stays on your iPhone

Your games, ratings against the computer rivals, streaks, puzzles, lessons and settings are stored on your device. Stockfish, the chess engine, runs on the device too. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What leaves your iPhone, and why

  • Photo of a board. When you take or pick a photo to analyze, it is sent to our scanner, which asks Anthropic's Claude to read the position. The photo is not stored after the position is read.
  • Talk It Through with Coach Leo. Your speech is turned into text on your iPhone. That text and the position go to our coach server, which asks Anthropic's Claude for Leo's answer and ElevenLabs for his voice. We don't keep what you said; our logs hold only timings and error codes.
  • Playing a friend or the online lobby. The name you choose, your moves and a random player key (which holds your online rating) are kept on our game server. Your name is shown to your opponent. Unjoined games expire after 7 days, idle games after 30 days, and finished games are deleted 30 days after they end.
  • Openings, endgames and imports. To choose a rival's move or show a tablebase result, the app sends the position (never anything about you) to Lichess. If you import your games, the username you type is sent to Chess.com or Lichess to fetch your public games.
  • Premium. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Our servers see your IP address, as every server does, and use it only to apply daily fair-use limits. It is not stored with anything else.

Camera, microphone and speech

The camera is used only when you photograph a board. The microphone and speech recognition are used only while you hold the button to talk to Leo, and recognition happens on your iPhone. You can turn any of these off in the iPhone's Settings.

Children

Beauty of Chess does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only thing anyone can enter is a display name for online games, which is filtered and can be reported.

Your choices

Every feature that uses a server is optional; you can play the rivals, train and solve puzzles entirely offline. Because nothing is linked to you, there is no account to delete; to ask about anything we hold, write to us and we'll help.
